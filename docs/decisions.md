| D25 | 2026-11-24 | Video (Phase 4): a teacher pastes a YouTube link or ID; the server checks it before attaching it (missing/private and non-embeddable videos are refused). Each teacher registers a video once (`VideoAsset`, reused across lessons). The player gets the provider and video ID from a separate playback endpoint (owner teacher or approved students only), which is where signed playback would plug in for another provider. | Approved (default, Phase 4) |
| D26 | 2026-11-24 | In-app player: the official YouTube embed in privacy-enhanced mode (youtube-nocookie.com) inside a WebView (`webview_flutter`), loaded with a `Referer` that identifies the app (`https://com.etutor.app/`, from the application ID) as YouTube requires; without it every video fails with error 152/153. YouTube's own controls (captions, quality, fullscreen); fullscreen switches to landscape. Chosen over `youtube_player_iframe` (5.x can't set the referrer separately from the player host; 6.x is deprecated and needs Flutter 3.38+) and its successor `youtube_player` (needs Flutter 3.50+). Revisit after a Flutter upgrade. | Approved (default, Phase 4) |
